## Primary ICP — "the hybrid regional"

**US regional asset carrier, 10–100 power units, with (or building) a brokerage arm.**

| Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|
| Size | 10–100 trucks (FMCSA band ≈ 42K carriers ≈ 7.2% of carriers — big enough to have back office, small enough to have no IT dept) |
| Revenue | ~$2M–$25M combined asset + brokerage |
| Ops profile | Regional/interline OTR; dry van / reefer / flatbed; LTL or partials a plus (interline logic is a differentiator) |
| Current stack | Legacy TMS (McLeod-class) or spreadsheets + QuickBooks; paper/photo PODs; factoring or 45–90-day terms |
| Structural tells | ≥1 office staff per ~8 drivers · factoring fees ≥2% · detention disputes lost monthly · owner personally chases PODs/invoices |
| Geography | US, lower-48; single- or few-terminal |

**Buying committee:** economic buyer = owner/GM (feels margin + cash); champion = ops/dispatch manager (feels the phone); must-not-hate = drivers (zero-instruction PWA is the adoption bar); influencer = the bookkeeper/factoring relationship.

**The three priced pains (the only three we lead with — each carries a research-backed number):**
1. **Detention money left on the dock** — 39.3% of stops detained; ~$340K/yr operational exposure at 25 trucks; 18% of drivers never see detention pay. SHUDDL: signed arrival/departure events = evidence-backed claims.
2. **The cash-cycle tax** — DSO ~47 days; factoring ~2.8%/invoice; a 10-truck fleet floats ~$900K. SHUDDL: invoice + evidence in the customer's inbox the second the POD is signed.
3. **Back office that eats the margin** — >⅓ of staff are non-drivers; ~$25–60 admin per load; sector operating margin −2.3%. SHUDDL: money is a projection of physics; re-entry ceases to exist.

**Jobs-to-be-done:** "When a driver gets a signature at a door, I want the money side already handled, so I stop paying people to re-type reality." · "When a customer disputes, I want to send proof, not have an argument."

## Secondary ICP — small/mid brokerage
≤ $30M gross, 5–50 seats, fraud-burned (22% of 3PLs lost >$200K in 6 months), quote-speed-sensitive (+9–11 pts win rate under 5 minutes). Enters through the brokerage arm of the primary, or standalone after Founding Carrier proof.

## Disqualifiers (say no fast)
- **>100 trucks / enterprise brokers** — wrong motion, wrong sales cycle, project44/LSP44 territory.
- **1–5 truck owner-operators as primary** — too little back office to save (they are the *driver-love* audience and future PLG, not Founding Carriers).
- **Anyone unwilling to run the 30-day shadow** beside their incumbent (genesis/13 calendar is non-negotiable).
- **Freight outside OTR trucking** (ocean/air/parcel/warehouse) — federation later, never a build now.
- **Seat-based-pricing shoppers** — register do-not-build; pricing is per-operation value.
