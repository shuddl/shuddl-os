## The three load-bearing decisions
1. **"Same transaction window" = a synchronous, queue-triggered SECOND append — not one DB transaction.** The POD append commits first; the sequencer enqueues the Biller trigger (via `ctx.waitUntil` after its own batch). The Biller then appends `invoice.issued` through the sequencer DO, where I2 + the money projection ARE atomic (one `db.batch`). Two events, two commits, causally chained — the "same second" is a fast idempotent second append, not a distributed transaction. The residual trigger-loss window between the two is closed by the WP-11 reconciliation sweep (REQ-169), safe because the whole pipeline is idempotent.
2. **The penny-parity source is the RECORDED quote's itemized lines — never a re-rate.** `quote.priced` now carries its itemized lines (REQ-003/031); `composeInvoice` projects the invoice verbatim from those recorded lines, so the invoice equals what the shipment was actually sold under, not what a re-run of today's rater would produce. No pricing logic (no LLM, no float) touches the money path here.
3. **Anomaly / below-floor ⇒ HOLD, always.** An anomalous total (the $222K case) or a below-floor executing share appends NOTHING and sends NOTHING — it holds for a human. Auto-invoicing a suspect number is the one thing the Biller must never do (REQ-040). Below-floor interline release via `approval.decided` is intentionally unwired (fail-closed) until the approvals queue lands (WP-10/11).

## Assumption log
- **Accepted quote = the latest `quote.priced` before the POD.** No booking/acceptance flow emits `quote.accepted` yet, so the Biller loads the most recent priced quote recorded before the POD's seq. When booking lands (WP-08), switch to the quote event the booking references.
- **NULL `bill_terms` → prepaid (documented default, not a fault).** The payer is terms-invariant today: `bill_to_party_id` is "who pays" under every terms value (`third_party` doubles it as `third_party_id`), so terms only label the document — holding over a NULL label would block real revenue. Unpick when booking populates terms and a distinct third-party column exists (WP-08). An UNRECOGNIZED non-NULL value is a DATA FAULT: log loud, proceed prepaid.
- **`signed_by` = "Signature on file".** The ledger records a signature HASH, not a printed name (`pod.signed.signature_hash`). The email says what is true; it does not fabricate a signer name. Thread a name through capture when the driver flow collects one.
- **Photos absent from the email (`photos: {}`).** Documentary placeholders until the R2-signed-URL resolver is wired (REQ-170). The email composes and sends without them.
- **The tenant executing party for the floor check = `pod.actor.party`.** An org party, device co-signed (I4) — the authoritative "who is the tenant here" for the interline executing-share comparison.

## WP-exit audit swarm (REQ-119)
A 3-lens adversarial swarm ran at exit — **money-integrity** (penny parity, interline floor, projection), **queue/idempotency/tenancy** (redelivery, poison, cross-tenant), and **evidence/email/design/leak** (render, byte-verify, identity). Each auditor attempted to BREAK a defect class and RAN the code to confirm. Every High was fixed and re-verified closed. **No open Criticals at close.**

**Found + CLOSED:**
- **HIGH — interline label fail-OPEN (REQ-040).** Classification keyed off the `interline` KIND: a partner leg recorded under `linehaul`/`cartage`/`dray` but carrying a real revenue split billed at full gross with the executing-share floor skipped (the $222K-class fail-open). → `resolveInterline` now classifies by the DATA (any `split_bps` or a second executor forces the share computation); DIRECT is only the no-split single-executor shape; any ambiguity HOLDS. Re-verified in `compose.test.ts` / `biller.test.ts`.
- **HIGH — direct `invoice.issued` append bypass (REQ-030).** A client could append `invoice.issued` (and money kin) through the public `POST /v1/events`, minting money with no POD. → The public events route refuses money-projection kinds; only the Biller's server-controlled DO path emits them.
- **MED — zero-cent line poison (REQ-003).** A zero-cent invoice line could ride the contract and project a degenerate money line. → Zero-cent lines are rejected at the contract boundary.
- **LOW — `inlineTokens` over-broad.** The email token-inliner matched too widely. → Scoped to styles only, so it can't rewrite content it shouldn't.

**Registered (recorded as new scope, not left as silent gaps):**
- **REQ-170** — the missing-evidence mitigation: a POD can still gate through with a fabricated hash and ZERO stored bytes, and the Biller currently sends the "this email is the record" email without checking that bytes were stored. The transition-gate residual prescribes a Biller-side "surface as MISSING" mitigation that is UNIMPLEMENTED (it pairs with the deferred photo-URL resolver). Annotated at both sites, gated WP-06 follow-up.
- **REQ-169** — the commit→enqueue trigger-loss window: a `pod.signed` whose Biller trigger is lost between commit and enqueue would never invoice. The idempotent pipeline lets a WP-11 cron sweep re-drive it. Registered to WP-11.

**Lows noted (non-blocking, no behavior change):**
- A `pod.signed` with no `shipment_id`/shipments row logs LOUD and skips as a data fault — but the REQ-169 sweep can't recover it (there is nothing to project from); recorded, not a Critical.
- A deterministic `composeInvoice` throw (a genuine data fault, not a hold) would burn the queue's 5 retries before the DLQ — noisy but bounded; the DLQ + exceptions surface land WP-11.

**Strong negatives (attacked hard, held):** money lines exist ONLY as projections of `invoice.issued` (the Biller writes no rows); Σ invoice === quote sell === money_lines, penny-exact and chain-verified; the $222K anomaly holds and never auto-invoices; interline bills the executing share on the DATA not the label; redelivery yields one invoice + one email (deterministic ids both sides); a poison/foreign POD is skipped, never appended; the invoice stands even when the send fails (REQ-031); the evidence email is design-law-clean and token-inlined for real mail clients.
