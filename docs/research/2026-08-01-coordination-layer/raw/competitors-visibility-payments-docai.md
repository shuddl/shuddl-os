## Cross-cutting observations (preserved)

1. **Consolidation around adjacent incumbents:** WiseTech→E2open (Aug 2025), DAT→Trucker Tools (Dec 2024) + Convoy Platform (Jul 2025) + Outgo (May 2025), Truckstop→Denim (Aug 2025), Triumph→Greenscreens (May 2025), Loop→Data2Logistics/StrategIQ (2025), Mudflap→Parade (2026). Independent mid-market tools keep getting absorbed by load boards and banks; the small-carrier/broker segment increasingly buys from three or four consolidators.
2. **Nobody in visibility owns physical truth at the door.** RTTV is shipper-funded surveillance with documented carrier resentment; FourKites exited documents entirely (Haven). A carrier-native co-signed event attacks the category's core resentment.
3. **TriumphPay is the money-network incumbent** (>50% of brokered TL), but audits after the fact — the POD-moment evidence gap is open.
4. **Pricing opacity is universal** except MacroPoint's per-load ladder ($0.10–$5/load) — a usable anchor for freight-event pricing.
5. **Open gaps:** TriumphPay Q1 2026 numbers (SEC 403); Relay Payments status; Extend; newer LLM-native freight doc scan.
