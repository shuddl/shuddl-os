## Cross-cutting findings (both agents, preserved)

1. **Consolidation into money-adjacent incumbents is the 2025-2026 pattern.** Greenscreens → Triumph (a bank), Parade → Mudflap (fuel fintech), Convoy tech + Outgo + Trucker Tools → DAT, Tabi ← LSG. Point solutions are being absorbed into platforms that monetize the money flow — validates "money as a projection of physics" but means incumbents are assembling the same bundle from parts.
2. **Every voice/email vendor is an overlay, not a system of record.** All pitch "plugs into your existing TMS"; two (HappyRobot, Vooma) use browser agents to scrape web UIs where APIs don't exist. None owns a ledger of what physically happened. Integration effort is real: Cargo Chief publicly cites 1-4 weeks per TMS; HappyRobot needs forward-deployed engineers.
3. **"AI agents" is table-stakes vocabulary but almost all of it is intake/communication automation** on a conventional mutable-database TMS. Nobody ships an append-only co-signed event ledger or same-second POD→invoice+evidence. No direct analog found in 2025-26 announcements.
4. **The direct collision course is Alvys** (hybrid carrier+broker, small/mid US, open API, fresh $40M); on language, Rose Rocket/TMS.ai and Loadsmart's "Freight OS."
5. **The digital-brokerage era formally ended in this window** — tech cannot subsidize brokerage margin through a freight recession; supports selling the OS to the physical operator rather than intermediating freight.
6. **Carrier-side hostility to AI calls is documented**: "If it's AI, I hang up — unless I'm desperate" (r/FreightBrokers via [FreightCaviar, Aug 14 2025](https://www.freightcaviar.com/stories/689d9e4ae52dd10001dcef50)).
7. **Nobody in voice/pricing targets SHUDDL's exact wedge** (small/mid regional carriers + brokerages wanting one system); voice vendors chase enterprise brokers; carrier-side AI is thin dispatch tooling on load boards.

**UNVERIFIED items:** FleetWorks $6/load; Tai Software funding; Truckbase funding; HappyRobot 150+ enterprise client breadth vs 70+ (two agents differ — treat as 70-150 range); Emerge distress rumors; Flock layoff exact date; G2 review deep-dives for voice vendors.
