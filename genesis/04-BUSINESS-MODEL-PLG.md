## 3. Network economics (the compounding layer)
- **SHUDDL Direct take**: brokered/guest bookings through the central network — standard brokerage gross margin, executed by tenant capacity (they earn linehaul; platform earns spread).
- **Instant settle fee (~1%)**: escrowed same-day settlement on network moves — half of factoring's 2–5%, funded by float + loss models on Passport data. This line item alone can dwarf SaaS revenue at scale.
- **Cartage mesh take (5–8%)** on final-mile/interline legs executed via the marketplace.
- **EDI partner activation** (flat, ~$500/partner) — priced at 1/10th the industry's "EDI project."
- **Passport verifications** (per pull) for credit/vetting decisions by third parties.
SaaS pays the bills; the network is the business. Every tenant makes Direct, settle, and the mesh more liquid (L9) — and the free tier feeds all three.
