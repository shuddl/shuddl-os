## (02) ICP v1 (R1–R2) — who buys the wedge
- **Firmographics:** US asset-based LTL and final-mile carriers, 10–150 power units, 1–6 terminals, ~$5M–$75M revenue, owner-led decisions. Secondary: hybrid carrier+brokerage (tenant-0 class). Explicitly NOT v1: Tier-1 LTL, pure TL spot brokers, forwarders, parcel.
- **Systems context:** legacy or homegrown TMS (McLeod, TMW, AS/400-era, spreadsheets, and tenant-0's incumbent — named only in the config pack). The overlay never asks them to leave it (L8 = the sales motion).
- **Qualifying pains (score ≥2 to pursue):** unbilled revenue discovered late or by accident · rating backlog / probills waiting on rates · POD disputes and "send me the POD" cycles · DSO > 45 days · month-end close > 5 business days · interline reconciliation by spreadsheet.
- **Trigger events:** TMS vendor acquired/re-priced (tenant-0's incumbent vendor was just PE-acquired — this window is live now) · auditor or factor flags documentation · a big unbilled discovery · new controller/GM hire.
- **Buying committee:** economic buyer = owner/GM · champion = controller (the wedge is an AR/evidence product — sell to the money desk) · users = billing desk, dispatch, drivers · likely blocker = ops manager fearing driver pushback → counter with the zero-instruction driver demo (L6 acceptance test, on video).

## (04) POSITIONING — one sentence per competitor class (falsifiable, keep current)
- **vs. the incumbent's PE-backed "AI-native" rebuild:** *Keep your TMS — we'll quietly out-prove it.* The Overlay runs beside it; the parity dashboard is the close. When the acquirer's integration friction bites (pricing, roadmap, support), the refugees already run half their ops on SHUDDL.
- **vs. Alvys / Rose Rocket / Tai (modern TMS):** they modernize the TMS; **we delete the reconciliation layer** — co-signed evidence, invoice in the same second as the POD, disputes answered by hash-chained packets. None of them emails the consignee a signed-POD photo with the invoice at the door.
- **vs. HappyRobot / Vooma / Augment (agent staffing):** they answer the phone; **we own the record** the phone call was about. Their agents can ride our MCP surface (they are channel, not competition, at R4).
- **vs. Vector / doc-capture / freight audit:** they capture and audit documents after the fact; **we gate custody at the moment of the event** so there is nothing to audit.
- **Category line for R1 (the wedge):** *"Your $402K problem, structurally extinct — unbilled alarms at ≠0, invoices the second the driver signs, evidence no one argues with. Keep your TMS."*

1. **Founder-led direct** (R1): 25-list outreach on the heartbeat video + parity dashboard screenshot; the demo IS the deck.

## (08) TRIPWIRES (pre-committed pivots — reviewed at the weekly register ritual)
- **No signed design partner within 8 weeks of M-H** → narrow the wedge (sell the unbilled alarm + evidence packet alone) and re-run the 25-list.
- **Prospects consistently ask for one subsystem** → productize that subsystem as its own SKU; do not insist on the bundle.
- **Overlay adapter work exceeds 2 weeks per new prospect TMS** → freeze ICP to the incumbent's installed base only until adapters are productized.
- **Tenant-0 authority flips slip two gates in a row** → pause all GTM spend; the product isn't ready to be sold, only built.
- **The incumbent's rebuild ships a credible evidence-ledger clone** → accelerate R4 network/anchoring (the part a TMS vendor structurally can't copy: cross-party settlement + portable Passports).
